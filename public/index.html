How to play

The goal

Two teams of two (you + the player across). First team to the target score wins. A deal is worth 162 points.

Bidding

One card is turned up. In turn, take it as trump or pass. If everyone passes, a second round lets you name any other suit. The taker's team must score more than the defenders, or they go dedans and lose everything.

Card order

Trump: J 20 · 9 14 · A 11 · 10 10 · K 4 · Q 3 · 8 · 7
Plain: A 11 · 10 10 · K 4 · Q 3 · J 2 · 9 · 8 · 7

Playing

Follow suit if you can. If trump is led you must play a higher trump when possible. If you cannot follow, you must trump — unless your partner is already winning the trick.

Bonuses

Belote & Rebelote — hold K+Q of trump and play both: +20, automatic.
Dix de der — last trick: +10.
Capot — all eight tricks: 252.

Controls

Touch: tap to lift a card, tap again or flick up to play.
